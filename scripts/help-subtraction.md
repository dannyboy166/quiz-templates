# Help Lesson Script: Subtraction

**Topic:** Subtraction
**Duration:** ~75 seconds
**Character:** Teacher (friendly, encouraging)

---

## SCRIPT

### INTRO (5 sec)

**[VISUAL: Teacher character appears]**

**TEACHER:** "Let's figure out what subtracting really means!"

---

### PART 1: Take Away (25 sec)

**[VISUAL: 7 cookies appear on a plate]**

**TEACHER:** "I have 7 cookies. Yum!"

**[VISUAL: A hand reaches in and takes 3 cookies - they float away or get eaten]**

**TEACHER:** "But I eat 3 of them..."

**[VISUAL: 4 cookies remain, number 4 appears]**

**TEACHER:** "How many are left? Let's count... 1, 2, 3, 4. I have 4 left!"

**[VISUAL: Show 7 - 3 = 4]**

**TEACHER:** "That's subtraction. We started with 7, took away 3, and 4 are left."

---

### PART 2: The Minus Sign (15 sec)

**[VISUAL: Focus on the equation 7 - 3 = 4]**

**TEACHER:** "This little line is called minus."

**[VISUAL: The minus sign pulses/glows]**

**TEACHER:** "Minus means 'take away'. Seven... take away... three... equals four."

---

### PART 3: Another Way to Think About It (20 sec)

**[VISUAL: Two kids - one with 5 stickers, one with 3 stickers]**

**TEACHER:** "But here's something cool. Subtraction isn't JUST taking away."

**[VISUAL: Highlight the difference - show the gap between 5 and 3]**

**TEACHER:** "Sam has 5 stickers. Mia has 3. How many MORE does Sam have?"

**[VISUAL: Show the 2 extra stickers that Sam has]**

**TEACHER:** "We're finding the DIFFERENCE. Sam has 2 more than Mia."

**[VISUAL: Show 5 - 3 = 2]**

**TEACHER:** "Same subtraction, different question!"

---

### SUMMARY (10 sec)

**[VISUAL: Split screen - "Take Away" on left, "Find the Difference" on right]**

**TEACHER:** "So subtraction can mean 'take away'... OR 'how many more'. Both use minus!"

**[VISUAL: Teacher gives thumbs up]**

**TEACHER:** "Now you know the secret!"

**[VISUAL: Fade out]**

---

## VISUAL ASSETS NEEDED

1. Teacher character (existing)
2. Cookie illustrations (7 cookies on a plate)
3. Hand/eating animation
4. Two kid characters with stickers
5. Number animations (7, 3, 4, 5, 2, -)
6. Comparison visual (showing the "gap" between two amounts)

## VOICE NOTES

- Keep it playful (cookies, stickers = relatable)
- Emphasise: "take away", "left", "difference", "more"
- The two meanings of subtraction is the key insight

## KEY INSIGHT

Kids often only learn subtraction as "take away" - but "finding the difference" is equally important and shows up in comparison word problems. This Help lesson teaches both.
