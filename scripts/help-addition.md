# Help Lesson Script: Addition

**Topic:** Addition
**Duration:** ~70 seconds
**Character:** Teacher (friendly, encouraging)

---

## SCRIPT

### INTRO (5 sec)

**[VISUAL: Teacher character appears, waves]**

**TEACHER:** "Hey! Let's learn what adding really means."

---

### PART 1: The Core Concept (20 sec)

**[VISUAL: 3 red apples appear on the left side of screen]**

**TEACHER:** "Addition is just... putting things together!"

**[VISUAL: 2 green apples appear on the right side]**

**TEACHER:** "Look - I have 3 apples here... and 2 apples here."

**[VISUAL: All 5 apples slide together into one group, with a gentle bounce]**

**TEACHER:** "When I push them together..."

**[VISUAL: The number 5 appears above the apples, maybe with a little sparkle]**

**TEACHER:** "...I have 5 apples altogether! That's adding."

---

### PART 2: Show It With Numbers (20 sec)

**[VISUAL: The apples fade, replaced by: 3 + 2 = ? ]**

**TEACHER:** "We write it like this. Three... plus... two."

**[VISUAL: The 3 pulses/highlights]**

**TEACHER:** "The 3 is my first group."

**[VISUAL: The 2 pulses/highlights]**

**TEACHER:** "The 2 is what I'm adding."

**[VISUAL: The + sign glows]**

**TEACHER:** "The plus sign means 'put them together'."

**[VISUAL: The ? transforms into 5 with a satisfying pop]**

**TEACHER:** "And we get... 5!"

---

### PART 3: The Trick (15 sec)

**[VISUAL: Show 2 + 3 = ? alongside 3 + 2 = 5]**

**TEACHER:** "Here's a cool secret..."

**[VISUAL: Arrows swap the 2 and 3, both still equal 5]**

**TEACHER:** "It doesn't matter which order! 3 plus 2, or 2 plus 3 - you get the same answer."

**[VISUAL: Both equations show = 5]**

**TEACHER:** "Because you're still putting the same things together."

---

### SUMMARY (10 sec)

**[VISUAL: Simple animation of two groups combining into one]**

**TEACHER:** "So remember - adding is just combining. Put the groups together, count how many altogether."

**[VISUAL: Teacher gives thumbs up]**

**TEACHER:** "You've got this!"

**[VISUAL: Fade out]**

---

## VISUAL ASSETS NEEDED

1. Teacher character (existing)
2. Apple illustrations (3 red, 2 green) - or any simple objects
3. Number animations (3, 2, 5, +, =)
4. "Combining" animation (two groups sliding together)
5. Highlight/pulse effect for emphasis
6. Sparkle/pop effect for the answer

## VOICE NOTES

- Warm, encouraging tone
- Pause slightly after questions
- Emphasise key words: "together", "altogether", "combining"
- Speak slowly - these are 5-7 year olds

## OPTIONAL EXTENSION

If you want a longer version (90 sec), add:
- A second example with different numbers (4 + 3)
- Show counting on fingers
- "Now you try!" moment before ending
